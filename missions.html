🚀 HISTORIC SPACE MISSIONS

Pushing the boundaries of human exploration across our cosmic neighborhood

Journey through humanity's most daring voyages into the deep cosmos — from early lunar touchdowns to interstellar deep-space probes, space observatories, and robotic rovers analyzing Martian soil.

1969 • NASA ● COMPLETED

🌕 Apollo 11

Carried Neil Armstrong, Buzz Aldrin, and Michael Collins to the Moon, achieving the very first human landing on another celestial world on July 20, 1969.

Destination: Earth's Moon | Payload: Lunar Module Eagle
1977 • NASA / JPL ● INTERSTELLAR

🛰️ Voyager 1 & 2

Conducted close flybys of Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune before crossing the heliopause into the uncharted void of interstellar space.

Destination: Outer Planets & Interstellar Space
1990 • NASA / ESA ● OPERATIONAL

🔭 Hubble Telescope

Revolutionized cosmology with crystal-clear deep-field photography, confirming the accelerated expansion of the universe and stellar nurseries.

Orbit: Low Earth Orbit (540 km) | Optics: 2.4m Mirror
2012 / 2021 • NASA ● SURFACE ACTIVE

🔴 Curiosity & Perseverance

Nuclear-powered robotic rovers conducting chemical spectroscopy, drilling ancient lake beds, and storing core samples in Jezero and Gale craters on Mars.

Destination: Martian Surface | Objective: Biosignatures
2021 • NASA / ESA / CSA ● OPERATIONAL

✨ James Webb Telescope

Stationed at the Sun-Earth L2 Lagrange point, JWST's gold-plated beryllium mirrors capture infrared light emitted just 300 million years after the Big Bang.

Location: Sun-Earth L2 (1.5M km) | Optics: 6.5m Array
2008 / 2023 • ISRO ● HISTORIC LANDING

🇮🇳 Chandrayaan & Mangalyaan

Confirmed water molecules on the Moon (Chandrayaan-1), achieved historic soft touchdown near the Moon's South Pole (Chandrayaan-3), and mapped Mars.

Destination: Lunar South Pole & Martian Orbit
