💼 SPACE SCIENCE & TECH CAREERS

Discover your pathway to shaping humanity's future in the cosmos

Space exploration is no longer limited to astronauts alone. It demands multi-disciplinary talent across astrophysics, propulsion robotics, machine learning, exobiology, and real-time mission telemetry.

Physical Sciences $125,000 – $180,000 / yr

🌌 Astrophysicist & Cosmologist

Analyzes spectral radiation, black hole thermodynamics, gravitational waves, and dark energy to formulate mathematical models of cosmological evolution.

Focus Areas: General Relativity, James Webb Spectral Data, Dark Matter Mapping
Key Skills: Quantum Mechanics • Python Astrophysics • Numerical Analysis
Rocketry & Hardware $115,000 – $175,000 / yr

🚀 Aerospace & Propulsion Engineer

Designs cryogenic rocket engines, ion thrusters, hypersonic thermal reentry shields, and structural aerodynamic airframes for launch vehicles and orbital landers.

Focus Areas: Stage Separation Dynamics, Cryogenic Propellants, Thermal Protection
Key Skills: Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) • Avionics • Thermodynamics
Earth Observation & Big Data $105,000 – $155,000 / yr

📡 Satellite Data & GIS Specialist

Processes synthetic aperture radar (SAR), multispectral imagery, and real-time orbital downlink telemetry for global climate modeling and planetary mapping.

Focus Areas: Planetary Surface Cartography, Ocean Thermal Scanning, Telemetry Pipelines
Key Skills: GIS Remote Sensing • Machine Learning • Satellite Telemetry Decoders
Life Sciences & Geochemistry $98,000 – $145,000 / yr

🦠 Astrobiologist & Planetary Scientist

Investigates extraterrestrial biosignatures, extremophile bacterial resilience in harsh vacuum conditions, and subsurface ocean chemistry on Europa and Enceladus.

Focus Areas: Mars Soil Regolith Bio-tests, Subsurface Ocean Habitability, Hydrothermal Vents
Key Skills: Extremophile Microbiology • Spectrometry • Planetary Mineralogy
Computer Science & Autonomy $130,000 – $195,000 / yr

🤖 Space Robotics & AI Developer

Architects autonomous hazard avoidance algorithms, computer vision perception, and terrain-relative navigation systems for Martian rovers and deep space probes.

Focus Areas: Rover Autonavigation, Robotic Arm Kinematics, Real-Time Radiation Hardening
Key Skills: C++ / ROS • Computer Vision • Embedded Real-Time Operating Systems (RTOS)
Operations & Systems Flight $110,000 – $165,000 / yr

🛰️ Flight Director & Mission Operations

Monitors deep-space orbital maneuvers, orbital station life support telemetry, communication blackouts, and crew safety in 24/7 mission command centers.

Focus Areas: Orbital Insertion Corridors, Telemetry Health Checks, Emergency Recovery
Key Skills: Systems Flight Architecture • Real-Time Crisis Management • Deep Space DSN
